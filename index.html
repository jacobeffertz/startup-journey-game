From Spark to Startup

The Founder's Journey — a week-by-week startup simulator. Run a company in Silicon Valley from a garage idea to an exit. Every week you decide where your time goes, what to build, who to hire and who to take money from. The market, your rivals and your own energy push back.

1 · What kind of venture is this?

Same idea, two different games. This is the first lesson of the course — pick the one you want to learn.

2 · Select your founder — Player 1

Six founders, six different starting positions. The bars are their starting stats — every trade-off is a number in your dashboard, not flavour text.

3 · What are you building?

Your sector changes what the market expects — regulated sectors pay more for compliance, crowded ones fight harder for customers.

A full run takes about 30–45 minutes. Nothing is saved to a server — your run lives in this browser.

·

4 · How a run ends — three ways out

You can finish the run any week, once you qualify. There is no single right ending.

💰 Sell the companyAn acquirer buys you out. You need 800 users first. The price grows with your users and revenue.
📈 Go public (IPO)List on the stock market. You need a Delaware C-Corp, $40,000 a month in revenue and 1,500 users.
🏠 Stay independentFinish as a profitable company you own outright. You need your monthly revenue above your monthly burn.

Build first, exit when you are ready. Every exit shows your final scorecard — the achievement card you can share.
