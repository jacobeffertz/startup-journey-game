    FIELD GUIDE — THE CONCEPTS BEHIND THE GAME

    What you are actually learning

    The journey (Laurel's flowchart)

    1 Idea & market validation — interview users, map competitors, prove the problem is worth solving. 2 Team & skill assessment — audit your gaps, bring in co-founders. Funding fork: Bootstrap (LLC, full control, customer revenue) or VC (Delaware C-Corp, angels + VCs + startup attorneys, equity for capital). 3 Build MVP & compliance — core features only, plus trademarks, patents, licences. 4 Launch & market testing — retention, CAC, churn. No product-market fit? Pivot loop back to Stage 3. 5 Scale & growth — board, C-suite, full-time team, Series A+. Founder grows into CEO or hires one. Exit: IPO, acquisition, or keep operating.

    Entrepreneurship ≠ startup concepts

    EntrepreneurshipStartup concepts
    Core questionHow do I create value from an opportunity?How do I find a scalable model before the money runs out?
    Who it's forAnyone: SMEs, employees, social innovatorsFounders of high-growth, often tech ventures
    FinancingBootstrapping, loans, grants, revenueAngel and VC equity, staged rounds
    FailureA risk to manageExpected and useful: fail fast, pivot
    SuccessViability, sustainability, impactTraction, growth rate, ability to raise

    Teach entrepreneurship skills and customer discovery to everyone. Save scaling, equity rounds and pitching for teams that actually aim for that path.

    Glossary

    MVP — minimum viable product: the smallest thing that tests your riskiest assumption. Product-market fit — users retain, pay and refer others. CAC — cost to acquire one customer. Churn — customers leaving. Runway — cash ÷ monthly burn, in months. Pivot — change the problem, customer or model and re-test; not the same as quitting. Burn — cash spent per month. Equity — ownership you trade for capital or talent. LLC vs Delaware C-Corp — LLC is simple and pass-through; a C-Corp is what institutional investors expect.

    The ecosystem you're playing in

    Silicon Valley ranks #1 worldwide (Startup Genome 2026), ecosystem value over $3 trillion, ~$92B of regional VC in 2025. Talent, capital, universities and networks are dense here — and access to them is still uneven, especially for early-stage founders. AI is concentrating more of the money in bigger rounds: opportunity and a crowded field at once.
